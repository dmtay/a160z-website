How We Cut HR Lead Research
Time by 80% Using AI Scraping

The Problem

A corporate training company in Petaling Jaya sells training programmes to companies across Malaysia. Their target audience: HR managers and HR directors — the people who sign off on staff training budgets.

The problem? Finding those people was a full-time job in itself.

Their sales team spent the bulk of their days doing manual research — combing through LinkedIn profiles, job portals, building directories, and company websites just to find the right contact name, their email, and their phone number. Each company took at least 15 minutes of manual research. With thousands of companies to target, the math was brutal.

The salespeople weren't selling. They were Googling.

"Our team was spending more time finding leads than actually talking to them. We needed a way to scale our prospecting without scaling our headcount."

What We Built

a160z built an AI-Powered HR Lead Crawling System — a fully automated workflow that turns a list of company names (or even building directory photos) into a structured spreadsheet of HR contacts, complete with names, job titles, LinkedIn profiles, and contact details.

The process is dead simple for the sales team to use:

  • Upload a list of company names to Google Sheets, or drop building directory photos into Google Drive.
  • The system automatically reads the input, identifies each company, and begins deep research across LinkedIn and job portals.
  • AI agents scrape, cross-reference, and extract HR-relevant contacts — HR Managers, HR Directors, L&D Executives — and filter out irrelevant results.
  • All findings are automatically written back to Google Sheets, clean and ready for outreach.

No login required. No manual steps. The team simply drops the input and comes back to a ready-to-use lead list.

The Solution Architecture

The n8n + Google Gemini workflow that automates HR contact discovery from LinkedIn and job portals

The system runs on n8n with Google Gemini as the AI model for intelligent contact extraction and relevance filtering. When a file is created or updated in Google Drive, the workflow triggers automatically — looping through each company, running web scraping sub-agents, and compiling results into a master Google Sheet.

The architecture includes multiple parallel AI agents, each specialised in a different data source, working simultaneously to speed up research time across large batches.
