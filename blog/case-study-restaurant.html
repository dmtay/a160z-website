The Problem

Picture a restaurant in Kuala Lumpur that does more than just walk-in dining. They take bookings for private events — company dinners, team celebrations, marriage proposals, birthday parties, anniversary nights. Sometimes a guest wants a single table tucked away. Sometimes a corporate client wants to book the entire restaurant exclusively for a product launch dinner.

Every single enquiry came in through WhatsApp. And every single one required a human to handle it.

The conversation would go back and forth: What date? How many guests? Which section — half the restaurant or the full venue? Do you want a custom menu? Can we add decorations? Each booking was a thread of 10 to 20 messages, manual calendar checks, and handwritten notes. Miss a message and you double-book. Forget to block the calendar and two events clash on the same night.

It was chaotic — and the owner was the one absorbing all of it.

"Every private event enquiry became a long WhatsApp conversation. We were spending more time negotiating bookings than actually preparing for the events."

What We Built

a160z built a WhatsApp AI Private Event Booking Agent — a conversational AI system that handles the entire enquiry-to-confirmation process automatically, no human intervention needed.

Here's how it works for a customer wanting to book a company dinner:

  • The customer messages the restaurant's WhatsApp number with their enquiry.
  • The AI agent responds naturally — asking for the event date, number of guests, seating preference (single table, multiple tables, half venue, or full exclusive closure), and any custom menu requirements.
  • It checks Google Calendar in real time to confirm availability for the requested date and configuration.
  • If available, it captures all booking details, confirms with the customer, and creates the calendar event automatically — with full details logged to Google Sheets.
  • If the date is unavailable, it suggests alternative slots and continues the conversation until a booking is locked in.

For a marriage proposal booking, the agent handles the intimacy of the request with the same care — capturing seating preferences, special decorations requests, and menu choices, all within WhatsApp. It even handles voice notes, transcribing audio messages in Mandarin, Malay, or English seamlessly.

The Solution Architecture

The n8n automation workflow powering the restaurant special event booking system

The workflow is built on n8n, an open-source automation platform, with Google Gemini as the AI brain. When a WhatsApp message arrives, it flows through a structured pipeline: language detection and transcription → information gathering → calendar availability check → booking confirmation or rescheduling suggestion.

Everything is logged automatically to Google Sheets for the owner to review at any time — a clean record of every booking, every cancellation, every message.
