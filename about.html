About a160z

We Started with One Belief:
AI Should Work for Businesses,
Not the Other Way Around.

Founded in March 2024, a160z was built to bridge the gap between powerful AI technology and practical, production-ready business automation.

Our Story

Born from Frustration. Built for Results.

Too many businesses were watching AI pass them by — not because they didn't want it, but because the gap between "AI is amazing" and "AI is running in my business today" was too wide to cross alone.

a160z was built to close that gap. We started in March 2024 with a simple mission: help SMEs and enterprises turn AI from a buzzword into a business tool that actually saves time, reduces headcount pressure, and drives real outcomes.

We don't sell AI concepts. We build AI systems — production-ready, integrated into your existing workflows, and running from day one.
