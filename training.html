FAQ

Common Questions

Do I need coding experience to join?
No coding experience is required for most of our programmes. We use visual, no-code tools like n8n that are designed for non-developers. A basic understanding of business processes is all you need.
Are the sessions conducted in English or Bahasa Malaysia?
Sessions are primarily conducted in English, with Bahasa Malaysia support available upon request. For corporate workshops, we're happy to accommodate your team's preferred language.
Can the corporate workshop be customised for our industry?
Absolutely — customisation is a core part of our corporate offering. We tailor the content, examples, and hands-on exercises to your specific industry, tools, and business processes.
What tools and software will I need?
For most programmes, you'll need a laptop with internet access. We'll guide you through setting up free accounts for n8n, Google Workspace, and any other tools required before the session begins.
Is there support after the training?
Yes. All participants get post-training support via WhatsApp for questions that come up as you apply what you've learned. Corporate clients also receive a documentation package and follow-up check-in session.
